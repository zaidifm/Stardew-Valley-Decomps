# Notice

This repository contains reverse-engineered and decompiled material derived from Stardew Valley binaries for research, interoperability, analysis, and tooling work.

It intentionally excludes original game distributions and content assets such as Steam game archives, IPAs, original managed/native binaries, XNB assets, maps, textures, and audio banks.

Stardew Valley and its original code/content are the property of their respective rights holders. Decompiled output is not represented as original source code. In particular, iOS Ghidra `.c` files are high-level pseudocode reconstructed from ARM64 machine code.
